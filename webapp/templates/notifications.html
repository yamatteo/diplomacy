On iPhone and iPad notifications only work once the site is on your home screen:

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen.
  2. Open Diplomacy from the new icon on your home screen and log in.
  3. Come back to this page (it is in the menu at the top) and tap Enable notifications.

This needs iOS 16.4 or later.

Notifications are blocked for this site. Allow them in your browser's site settings (on Android: tap the icon left of the address, then Permissions; on iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Diplomacy), then reload this page.
